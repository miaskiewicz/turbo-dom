## Historical component receipt — 2026-11-18

The receipt at
`tools/component-parity/evidence/v0.8.5-validation-2026-11-18.json` explicitly
records runtime `b899b750c46e5da9035026026687e44ca2fef2a2`. Its component inputs,
geometry and screenshot tolerances were unchanged. All twelve text-free reductions
at the three widths had zero changed pixels under the original harness threshold.
Those results predate the additional fixes below; they are not final-head CI or a
new full-component pixel-parity result.

The complete components also exposed real paint defects: spaced functional border
colors fell back to text color, inline SVGs lost inherited presentation, and alpha
border bands overlapped at corners. These have dedicated independent Chromium
controls and integrated corrections below. Remaining full-component pixel differences
must still be separated into semantic defects and platform font/raster sampling;
geometry agreement alone does not establish pixel-perfect arbitrary-page rendering.

## Performance

The historical 2026-11-18 paired 15-block audit and complete output hashes are
committed in
`tools/perf/healthy-flex-grid-v0.8.5-2026-11-18.{json,md}`. Healthy definite flex/grid
are roughly neutral within noise (+0.32%/+0.65% paired medians); no broad layout or
PDF speedup is established. The large gains are isolated custom-property override
microbenchmarks (+39.44% payload, +13.79% theme), not full-page claims.

Those measurements predate the additional frame, SVG and border corrections above.
They must not be presented as a final-head benchmark; the combined runtime needs a
fresh paired measurement before making current performance claims.
