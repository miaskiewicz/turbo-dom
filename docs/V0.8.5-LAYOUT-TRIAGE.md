## Historical component receipt — 2026-11-18

The receipt at
`tools/component-parity/evidence/v0.8.5-validation-2026-11-18.json` explicitly
records runtime `b899b750c46e5da9035026026687e44ca2fef2a2`. Its component inputs,
geometry and screenshot tolerances were unchanged. All twelve text-free reductions
at the three widths had zero changed pixels under the original harness threshold.
Those results predate the additional fixes below; they are not final-head CI or a
new full-component pixel-parity result.

The complete components also exposed real paint defects: spaced functional border
colors fell back to text color, inline SVGs lost inherited presentation, and alpha
border bands overlapped at corners. These have dedicated independent Chromium
controls and integrated corrections below. Remaining full-component pixel differences
must still be separated into semantic defects and platform font/raster sampling;
geometry agreement alone does not establish pixel-perfect arbitrary-page rendering.

## Historical primary proof and later local consumer validation

The rebuilt Surf NAPI integration ran the actual dotLottie 0.70.0 JavaScript and
WASM player against turbo-dom `8c7f96adf8c55fdabad9b9c0d02fba0e0ff8af05`.
It reached `ready` through a genuine `load` event at frame 30, with 544,214
nontransparent canvas pixels and no page errors. The native PNG matched the
Chromium 156.0.8078.47 PNG with zero changed pixels; the release owner also
visually inspected the result. See the [proof receipt](../tools/component-parity/evidence/lottie-native-v0.8.5/proof.json),
[native PNG](../tools/component-parity/evidence/lottie-native-v0.8.5/native.png) and
[Chromium PNG](../tools/component-parity/evidence/lottie-native-v0.8.5/chromium.png).
This is a pinned primary-path result, not proof of every page or the final runtime
head. The Surf host changes in that run were uncommitted and their input/vendor
identity was retained as 35 SHA-256 hashes; the addon SHA-256 is
`4d0c17d0090bf071d835d899d97480f350c930b4044726b21e80abf65f193b1a`.

The runtime corrections for #518–#523 and #525–#534 are integrated. Private
`canvasHost.restoreJSON` is integrated as `019dd016`, adding a fifth canonical
canvas method. It accepts one strict full RGBA8/sRGB seed, performs validation and
pixel allocation before commit, preserves context identity on success and prior
state on failure, and resets drawing defaults/path/save state. It is not a generic
operation replayer. See [IMAGEDATA-523-INTEGRATION.md](IMAGEDATA-523-INTEGRATION.md)
for the complete transfer contract.

The later real native player run against `019dd016` again reported load/ready,
frame 30, no page errors and 544,214 nontransparent pixels, with zero changed pixels
across 810,000 compared screenshot pixels. Its consumer receipt will be retained
after the Surf changes are committed; do not relabel the historical 8c artifacts
as this newer run. Local Surf source-consumer validation passed nine MCP tests
(including four previous failures), 39 focused tests, 834 shim tests and 55
end-to-end tests. This validates the local MCP handoff under
[turbo-surf#527](https://github.com/miaskiewicz/turbo-surf/issues/527), not a
published consumer release.

The earlier CI coverage receipt at `5ed0ed7d` required follow-up fixes. At the
reported `e0a81a80` CI checkpoint, 21 checks were green, including all coverage
jobs, while five native jobs remained pending. That checkpoint predates the
combined typed-edge integration `2ba05e87` and does not validate the newly merged
source. New-source CI is still required.

Local strict and no-default checks pass. The full all-feature rerun with host GPU
access remains pending after the sandbox did not expose a Metal device. Surf's
`019dd016` source-consumer integration has green full Clippy and the focused,
MCP, shim, end-to-end and real-player checks above. Its whole-workspace run had
1,409 passing cases at the reported checkpoint, with render binaries still
running (160/303 reported); it is not a completed workspace result. No
completed final validation, PR, tag or publication is claimed. No clipping,
fixed offsets, new ignored tests, weaker floors or enlarged tolerances conceal
the retained comparisons.

## Performance

The admitted [combined candidate report](benchmarks/cascade-v085-bcfce3b4.md)
and [paired data](benchmarks/cascade-v085-bcfce3b4.json) measure
`bcfce3b4f31824e8b18293d7c795c4d11f8605a9` against v0.8.4. That validated bundle
was integrated as `2ba05e87`. Fifteen alternating paired blocks support modest
fixture-specific layout improvements:

| Workload | Baseline median ms/op | Candidate median ms/op | Paired improvement | Faster blocks |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 100-row report | 5.3542 | 5.3078 | +1.16% | 14/15 |
| 10-row report | 0.5741 | 0.5676 | +1.38% | 14/15 |
| 100 cards | 4.2787 | 4.2002 | +1.59% | 14/15 |
| Article | 2.2867 | 2.2718 | +0.86% | 10/15 |
| Healthy definite flex | 1.5917 | 1.5944 | +0.03%; effectively flat | 8/15 |
| Healthy definite grid | 1.5930 | 1.5895 | +0.53%; small result | 11/15 |

Paired percentages are medians of block ratios, not ratios of the separately
reported medians above. All 15 benchmark output hashes match v0.8.4. The 24
component PNG byte and geometry comparisons preserve the corrected pre-optimization
rendering; this is not a claim of perfect Chromium pixels for every component.
The performance measurements compare the candidate with v0.8.4; they establish
neither a broad guaranteed speedup nor a jsdom,
jsPDF or PDF-generation comparison. Isolated cascade microbenchmarks have
different units and are not document-layout gains.

The failed [e1516f2a checkpoint](benchmarks/cascade-v085-e1516f2a.md) and
[standalone 8234d47f experiment](benchmarks/cascade-v085-8234d47f.md), together

3.04%/2.17% slower; the standalone experiment measured them 3.89%/1.93% slower.
`8234d47f` was not admitted or merged on its own. Its borrowed-value/early-guard
ideas are included in the final typed-edge bundle, admitted only after the combined
measurement. Separate timing sessions do not establish the causal contribution
