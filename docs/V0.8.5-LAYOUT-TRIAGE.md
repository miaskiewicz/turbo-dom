## Additional defects found during validation

These are separate pre-existing reductions, not substituted native expectations:

- [#525](https://github.com/miaskiewicz/turbo-dom/issues/525): Taffy retains a gutter
  beside collapsed auto-fit tracks. All six independent diagnostic cases remain in
  the grid oracle; desktop/tablet reproduce the issue. See GRID-TRACK-SIZING-521.md.
- [#526](https://github.com/miaskiewicz/turbo-dom/issues/526): negative outer margins
  overcount the enclosing auto-height flex column by 150px while child positions
  agree exactly. The text-free fixture and both measurements are retained in
  tools/component-parity/evidence/negative-column-margins. This accounts for the
  remaining partner-container height discrepancy.

Both #525 and #526 are now assigned to v0.8.5 and are being corrected as part
of this release work. No clipping, fixed offsets,
new ignored tests, weaker floors or enlarged tolerances conceal these differences.

## Performance

The paired 15-block audit and complete output hashes are committed in
`tools/perf/healthy-flex-grid-v0.8.5-2026-11-18.{json,md}`. Healthy definite flex/grid
are roughly neutral within noise (+0.32%/+0.65% paired medians); no broad layout or
PDF speedup is established. The large gains are isolated custom-property override
microbenchmarks (+39.44% payload, +13.79% theme), not full-page claims.
